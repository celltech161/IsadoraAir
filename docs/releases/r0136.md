# r0136: Short end-of-hour legacy live-fill correction

r0136 makes a deliberately small improvement to the existing legacy live filler. When the active queue will end
slightly before the next hour, a shortage greater than 20 seconds and no greater than 30 seconds may now be closed
with eligible material from the configured fallback Category. Previously the ordinary fill path stopped at its
30-second duration-fit margin, so a late next-hour build could leave a short gap.

This is not a scheduling redesign. Scheduled-log construction, next-hour queue adoption, crossfade and mix-out,
Weather and urgent insertion, Active Messages, OGRemote, and Roadmap 2.27 Hour Refill retain their existing policy.

## What changes

- The engine calculates the authoritative shortage from exact wall time minus committed future runway before deciding
  whether the ordinary or closing path applies.
- A closing fill is eligible only when the deficit is greater than the accepted 20-second minimum and no greater than
  30 seconds.
- Closing selection uses effective airtime and accepts only material that has no planned overshoot beyond the top of
  the hour. A 30-second PSA is therefore not selected for a true 29.75-second shortage.
- A safe closing proposal may still install during the final 30 seconds. Its absolute top-of-hour deadline and the
  current committed runway are checked again before database or queue adoption.
- A closing proposal cannot extend a later hour that the engine has already adopted. The guard is memory-only and
  preserves current-hour, blank-hour continuation, midnight, partial-hour and DST fall-back behavior.
- An unused PSA appended shortly before rollover is acceptable. If the approved new-hour queue supersedes it before
  it airs, the row remains truthful history and is not replayed as part of the new hour.

## What remains unchanged

- Normal legacy fill above 30 seconds remains unchanged apart from using the exact sub-second shortage as its target.
  This can reduce the target by less than one second but does not relax its selection rules.
- `LogFillConfig`, including its configured PSA fallback Category, is unchanged. No new Category, priority or
  eligibility policy is introduced.
- The next-hour queue remains authoritative when adopted. Existing crossfade and mix-out behavior is unchanged.
- Weather alert production, NWS or AMBER ingestion, validity, urgency and playback are unchanged. r0136 does not fix
  missing or expired Weather alerts and makes no such claim.
- Native Active Messages and OGRemote are unchanged.
- Roadmap 2.27 Hour Refill remains unchanged and is not armed or activated by this release.

## Deferred nonblocking follow-up

The pre-existing exhausted-queue recovery limitation remains. If the final item ends unexpectedly and the next-hour
log is unavailable, `_on_log_exhausted` can clear the active queue while a reactive live-fill proposal is pending;
that proposal is then correctly rejected as stale. This behavior exists in r0135 and is independent of the proactive
closing-fill improvement. It is a deferred follow-up, not part of r0136.

## Validation

- The end-of-hour regression module passed 32/32 tests.
- The focused playout, asynchronous fill, rollover, DST, Manual, shutdown, r0135-continuity and Hour Refill set passed
  875 tests.
- The full Library comparison ran 3,284 tests with the same 73 inherited errors as r0135. The broader suite is not
  represented as green; those media-validation and sandbox errors are unchanged by r0136.
- Django system checks and migration-drift checks were clean.

The regressions cover final-30-second dispatch and installation, fractional thresholds, asynchronous callback
completion, database append, queue adoption, duplicate suppression, no-fit suppression, midnight and DST fall-back.

## Deployment declaration

There are no migrations, migration companion, packages, Python requirements, collectstatic, nginx, systemd or
protected-runtime changes. `protected_runtime` is `null`.

Required restarts, in canonical order: `isadoraair-gunicorn`, then `isadoraair-engine`. Both changed production
modules are reachable from Gunicorn, and the engine loads both directly. No other long-running service loads the
changed production surface.

An engine restart interrupts program audio and may end an active Remote DJ session. Publication does not authorize
installation at KOGR or WRJE; each station needs a separate preflight and authorization.
